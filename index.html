Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Naity ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Naity mobile application (the "App"), which provides mock speaking tests. By using the App, you agree to the practices described herein.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Name and email address – for account creation and communication.
  • Microphone – to capture spoken responses during mock tests.
  • Usage data – device type, OS version, app version, crash reports, and analytics to improve performance.

Recordings are used solely within the App and are not shared with third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide and improve the mock test experience
  • Analyse usage to enhance features and performance
  • Respond to support requests
  • Send important App updates (if you have opted in)

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data against unauthorized access. Any data transmitted to our servers is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data only with trusted service providers operating the App under confidentiality obligations, when required by law, or in the event of a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your personal data, or to object to its processing. This includes rights under GDPR (EEA) and CCPA (California). To exercise any right, contact us at naityapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted their data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as analytics and cloud infrastructure providers. These services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by a revised Effective Date. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

This Partner Affiliate Agreement (the "Agreement" or "Public Offer") is a public offer by the Naity application (the "Company") addressed to any individual or legal entity (the "Partner") willing to participate in the Naity Affiliate Program.

Acceptance of this Public Offer is made exclusively by email confirmation. The Agreement is considered accepted only upon the Partner sending an email to naityapp@gmail.com expressing their intent to participate, and the Company's written reply confirming the partnership and the individually agreed terms.

Acceptance by any other means — including use of a promo code, verbal agreement, or any other action — does not constitute conclusion of this Agreement.

2. Subject of the Agreement

The Partner undertakes to promote the Naity application among their audience, and the Company undertakes to pay remuneration for purchases made by users using the Partner's personal promo code.

The Company assigns each Partner a unique personal promo code that users may enter in the app to receive a discount on the purchase of attempts.

The specific discount amount for users and the remuneration percentage for the Partner are agreed individually between the parties prior to commencement of cooperation and are documented in writing (including by email or messenger).

3. Partner's Rights and Obligations

The Partner is entitled to: receive a personal promo code; receive remuneration for qualifying purchases; request statistics on purchases made using their code; and terminate this Agreement at any time with written notice.

The Partner undertakes to: promote Naity honestly without misleading users; not use spam or prohibited advertising methods; not transfer their promo code to third parties; not create fake purchases or artificially inflate statistics; and comply with applicable advertising laws.

4. Company's Rights and Obligations

The Company undertakes to provide a unique promo code in a reasonable time, ensure accurate tracking of qualifying purchases, pay remuneration per Section 5, and notify the Partner of material changes.

5. Remuneration and Payment

The Partner's remuneration is calculated as a percentage of net revenue from purchases made using the Partner's promo code. The specific percentage is agreed individually between the parties before commencement of cooperation.

"Net revenue" means the amount actually received by the Company after deduction of payment processing fees, applicable taxes, and refunds.

Payments are made upon reaching the minimum payout threshold of USD 100.00. Accrued remuneration below the threshold carries over to the following period.

The payment method is agreed individually between the parties. Payment is made within 14 business days from the end of the reporting month in which the threshold was reached. All taxes on the remuneration are the sole responsibility of the Partner.

A purchase is "qualifying" if: (a) the user entered the promo code at checkout; (b) payment was successfully completed; and (c) the purchase was not subsequently refunded.
